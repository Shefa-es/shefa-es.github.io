Who we are

A readiness-to-implementation venture for cross-border healthcare innovation.

SHEFA prepares selected biomedical, MedTech and healthtech innovations for responsible institutional evaluation and implementation pathways through readiness assessment, institutional translation and structured execution.

Built through European expertise and deep understanding of the UAE healthcare context, SHEFA creates structured pathways between innovation, evidence, institutional evaluation and responsible implementation.

Current Biomedical Opportunity Domains

SHEFA’s early readiness work spans selected biomedical opportunities across surgical MedTech, advanced surgical preparation, specialised clinical pathways and emerging translational science. SHEFA does not own the underlying technologies; its role is to structure the readiness and implementation pathway around them.

01

Surgical MedTech & Perioperative Intelligence

Readiness and implementation questions around surgical safety, procedural coordination, traceability and institutional integration.

Advanced Surgical Preparation

Emerging Biomedical & Translational Science

Responsible exploration of emerging biomedical opportunities where scientific fit, evidence maturity and governance must be assessed before implementation is considered.
